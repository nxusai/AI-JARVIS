# Meta Ads creative playbook for NXUS AI (2025-2026): video, hooks, CTWA, AI creative, Andromeda diversity

Research date: 2026-11-06. Labels: **[DATA]** = measured study/benchmark (note who measured it); **[VENDOR]** = vendor/agency claim with undisclosed method; **[OPINION]** = practitioner guidance; **[INFERENCE]** = my reasoning applied to NXUS AI. Most of the "creative" literature comes from vendors selling creative tools or agency services, and is DTC/ecommerce-weighted. Treat every benchmark as directional; NXUS should judge on its own cost per *qualified* diagnostic booked.

## Hooks: what works in the first 1-3 seconds; benchmarks for hook rate / thumb-stop

### Takeaway
Hook rate (3-s video plays / impressions on Meta) is the main early diagnostic. ~25% is the line between weak and workable; 30-35%+ is good, and Reels medians run higher than Feed. The best-supported hook guidance: within about 1 second show *who it is for / what the problem is*, add motion in the first half-second, make the message readable without sound, and test 5-10 opening variants on the same body.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA, aggregated by vendor] Hook rate = 3-s views ÷ impressions. Under ~20% weak; ~20-25% workable (Feed median ~23%); 25-35% solid; 35-45% strong; 45%+ exceptional. Reels medians run higher (~30%), so compare within placement. Under 25% is described as the "kill zone" for replacement. Hold rate (15-s views ÷ 3-s plays): ~25% minimum, 40-50% average, 60%+ strong. ThruPlay rate is ~15-30% on 15-30 s ads (median ~22%). — [Growth Spree, "Meta Video Ads for B2B SaaS (2026)", Oct 27 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)
- [OPINION] Open with the problem, a specific or surprising claim, a visible payoff, or motion. No logo or slow intro. The opening should identify the product within ~1 s, show the benefit without sound, and have motion in the first 0.5 s. Test 5-10 variants of only the first 2-3 seconds while keeping the body fixed. — [Growth Spree, Oct 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)
- [OPINION/benchmarks] Motion's glossary calls 20-40% thumbstop "generally solid". Other blogs put the elite tier at 35-45%+, and sources disagree. TikTok's metric uses a 2-s threshold and reads structurally higher than Meta's 3-s metric, so don't mix the two. — [Motion glossary](https://library.motionapp.com/glossary/thumbstop-rate); [Motion: key creative metrics](https://motionapp.com/blog/key-creative-performance-metrics); [Jetfuel 2026 benchmarks](https://jetfuel.agency/creative-performance-metrics-for-dtc-video-ads-hold-rate-thumbstop-and-thruplay-benchmarks-2026/)
- [OPINION, secondary] Hormozi structures an ad as Hook → "meat" (value) → CTA and reportedly says the hook carries most (~80%) of an ad's effectiveness. He recommends writing many hooks (e.g., 30 hooks × 10 bodies) and mostly reusing proven hook patterns while reserving a share for new ones. The "callout" ("If you are [audience], this is for you") is a core hook type. These come from summaries, not primary video. — [Tubegraph summary of Hormozi video](https://tubegraph.vercel.app/alexhormozi/effective_ad_creation_and_optimization_techniques); [Gist summary: 8 paid ad strategies](https://gist.ly/youtube-summarizer/alex-herozis-8-paid-ad-strategies-to-scale-fast); [hormozi-hooks skill page](https://skillselion.com/skills/alexsmedile/hormozi-skills/hormozi-hooks)
- [VENDOR, secondhand] Motion is said to report that 73% of ecommerce video ads "fail in the first three seconds because they look like ads", and to list pattern interrupts, curiosity gaps and social proof as top hook frameworks for 2025. I could not trace this to a primary Motion report. — via search summary of [Hawky](https://hawky.ai/blog/hook-rate) / [AdMake](https://admakeai.com/blog/hook-rate)
- [OPINION] Diagnose by funnel stage. A low hook rate means fix the opening. A good hook with poor hold means fix the middle. Good watch time with low CTR means fix the CTA or offer. Good CTR with bad lead quality means fix the offer or targeting, not the video. — [Growth Spree, Oct 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)

### Inferences (evaluation of NXUS hooks A/B/C)
- **Hook A, "Son las 11:48 de la noche… tu negocio está cerrado… y tus clientes te siguen escribiendo"**: a strong, specific scene with a curiosity gap, and it fits restaurants exactly. Risk: the ellipsis pacing delays the payoff past 3 s, and as voiceover alone it is invisible with sound off. Fix: from frame 1 show a real phone lock screen at "11:48 PM" with 5-10 WhatsApp notifications piling up ("¿Tienen mesa para 8 mañana?", "¿Hacen envío a Polanco?"), with burned-in text "11:48 PM. Cerrado. 14 mensajes sin responder." The visual alone should tell the story.
- **Hook B, "Este empleado atiende a tus clientes a las 3 AM… y nunca pide horas extra"**: a classic pattern-interrupt/reveal. It works if the "empleado" visual is surprising, for example a human-looking employee who turns out to be a WhatsApp chat. The "nunca pide horas extra" angle can read as anti-worker to some Mexican owners and staff. Test a variant framed as "tu equipo descansa; el negocio sigue vendiendo".
- **Hook C, "¿Tienes un restaurante o una empresa? Esto te está costando dinero"**: the callout format is the most directly supported (Hormozi callout). But "o una empresa" dilutes the callout, and "esto te está costando dinero" is generic. Sharpen it to a single audience per ad: "Si tienes 3 o más sucursales de restaurante en CDMX…" or "Dueños de restaurantes: ¿cuántos pedidos de WhatsApp pierden después de las 10 PM?" Specific numbers and places are what make a callout land.
- All three rely on the voiceover. Burn in large captions (2-5 words per beat) and make the first frame a readable text-plus-visual statement.

### Gaps
- No primary Motion report with hook-rate benchmarks specific to B2B services or lead gen was found. Benchmarks are DTC/SaaS aggregates.
- No public data was found on hook rates for Spanish-language or Mexico-specific ads.

## Ideal length, structure, captions, sound-off, 9:16 safe zones

### Takeaway
For cold traffic, the core message should land within 15 s. A 6-15 s cut is the suggested starting point for prospecting, and 16-30 s suits warm and retargeting audiences. NXUS's 32-34 s ads are long for cold traffic. Make 15 s cuts and keep the 30 s+ versions for retargeting and 25%-viewer audiences. Burn in captions, keep text and the CTA out of the bottom ~35% and top ~14% of 9:16, and use Ads Manager's safe-zone preview.

### Cited Findings
- [OPINION] Structure: hook 0-3 s → problem and payoff 3-15 s → proof or demo 15-30 s → CTA. Starting lengths: cold 6-15 s (judged on hook rate and CPL), warm 16-30 s (hold rate and CPL), retargeting 16-30 s (conversion rate). ThruPlay counts at 15 s. People who watched 25%+ make a good warm retargeting and lookalike seed. — [Growth Spree, Oct 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)
- [OPINION] Most viewing is muted, so burn in captions or on-screen text. Visuals should carry the message, with voiceover as a bonus. Use 9:16 for Reels/Stories and 1:1 or 4:5 for Feed. — [Growth Spree, Oct 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)
- [Meta guidance, via secondary] Meta recommends 9:16 for Stories, Status and Reels. Meta's Reels ads page is widely cited as reporting **34.5% lower cost per result vs image ads** for campaigns using 9:16 video with audio and key messages within the safe zone. I could not fetch Meta's page directly (it returned a login wall), so verify the figure. Captions are recommended but not required. — [WebsiteBuilderExpert on Meta's Reels guide](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/news/meta-updates-reels-ads-guide/); [Coinis how-to](https://coinis.com/how-to/design-facebook-reels-ad)
- [OPINION] Safe zones: one guide gives ~14% top, ~35% bottom and ~6% sides free of key text on 1080×1920. Reels' bottom third holds the account name, caption, audio attribution and like/comment/share icons. Ads Manager has a "Show safe zones" preview toggle. Sources disagree on exact margins. — [House of Marketers safe-zone guide](https://www.houseofmarketers.com/guide-to-safe-zones-tiktok-facebook-instagram-stories); [Reap safe zones](https://reap.video/blog/short-form-video-safe-zones)

### Inferences
- Build each NXUS concept as three cuts: a ~12-15 s cold cut (hook, one pain, one proof shot of the WhatsApp agent, CTA), the existing ~30 s version for warm/retargeting, and a 6 s bumper of hook plus CTA.
- Put the CTA ("Agenda tu diagnóstico gratis") as on-screen text in the middle band, not the bottom third, because the Reels UI covers the bottom.
- Move the brand or proof forward. A 30-s ad that only states the offer at the end loses everyone who drops off at 15 s (and most do).

### Gaps
- Meta's own Reels best-practice page was not retrievable (login wall). The 34.5% figure is secondhand.
- No B2B-services-specific length test data was found.

## Founder-led / UGC / talking-head vs polished AI/stock video; AI voices; Meta "AI info" labels

### Takeaway
Practitioner consensus for B2B services and agencies favors native, low-production formats: a founder or expert talking head, screen-recorded product demos, and client testimonials over glossy or stock brand films. Hard numbers are vendor-sourced and inconsistent. Consumer research from 2025 repeatedly finds that ads *perceived* as AI-made lose trust, credibility and memorability. One 2026 study (commercially linked) found that an explicit label did not hurt ad performance. Meta applies "AI info" labels to ads made or altered with its generative tools, always shown next to "Sponsored" when photorealistic humans are AI-generated. An all-AI ad with an AI voice ("Mark") and AI/stock people is the riskiest format for a high-ticket trust sale.

### Cited Findings
- [OPINION] For B2B on Meta, favor crisp product demos, founder or expert talking-head explainers and customer testimonials. Avoid glossy brand films; clarity and a strong hook outperform production value. — [Growth Spree, Oct 2026](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)
- [VENDOR] A B2B SaaS write-up reports a rough founder clip beating polished creative in its test. One lead-gen agency says the founder talking head usually wins on lead *quality* even with a slightly higher CPL. DTC-focused claims of founder-led creative 31-44% lower CPA or 2-3x better don't agree with each other and are unverified. — [Growth Spree TikTok vs Meta B2B](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/tiktok-ads-vs-meta-ads-for-b2b-saas-2026-the-benchmarks-a-real-b2b-test-and-how-to-use-each.md); [MHI founder-led guide](https://mhigrowthengine.com/blog/founder-led-video-ads-complete-guide/); [Elevarus lead-gen creatives 2026](https://elevarus.com/meta-ad-creatives-lead-generation-2026/)
- [VENDOR/talk] Motion keynote (Dec 2025) on creator-led B2B ads cites a Meta study with "up to 32%" efficiency gains from creator-led B2B ads. The underlying study was not verified. — [Motion talk: creator-led strategy boosting B2B ad efficiency 32%](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/the-creator-led-strategy-boosting-b2b-ad-efficiency-32-meta-study/)
- [OPINION, unverified] Polished creative may win by 10-20% for products over $200 and for feature breakdowns and premium positioning. Sourcing is unclear. — [UGC vs polished, Jun 2025](https://thread-trasferv2.pages.dev/blog/2025-06-24-ugc-vs-polished-creative)
- [DATA] NIQ neuroscience study (>2,000 participants, 2025): consumers detected AI-made ads, rated them less engaging, and showed weaker memory activation, and low-quality visuals distracted from the message. — [ESM Magazine on NIQ study](https://esmmagazine.com/a-brands/ai-generated-ads-may-have-a-negative-effect-on-consumer-perceptions-279327)
- [DATA, survey] Harris Poll / 4As / Infillion (Cannes 2025): 73% would be less likely to trust an ad they suspected was AI-made, and 63% less likely to buy from a brand using AI-generated ads. — [Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/harris-poll-ai-fatigue-less-trust-ai-generated-ads-cannes-lions)
- [DATA, experiment] NIM (2025): identical ads labeled "AI-generated" were rated less credible, less emotional and less memorable than ones credited to a photographer. "Transparency alone does not create trust." — [NIM](https://www.nim.org/en/publications/detail/transparency-alone-does-not-create-trust)
- [DATA, survey] Attest: US consumers objecting to AI in ads fell from 49% (2024) to 46% (2025). Acceptance skews young: 18% of 18-29 year-olds vs 1% of 65+ say they're more likely to buy (CivicScience). — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/many-us-adults-avoid-distrust-brands-that-use-ai-ads); see also [eMarketer: lack of disclosure is the problem](https://www.emarketer.com/content/ai-ads-isn-t-problem-most-consumers-lack-of-disclosure)
- [DATA, conflict-of-interest noted] MediaScience with Ehrenberg-Bass and MediaPET.ai (May 2026, n=900 US): AI labels caused no significant difference vs a no-label control on brand choice, ad liking, brand attitude or perceived production quality. A 3-s label raised AI awareness by 28%, and a full-duration label by 36%. Respondents most want labels when AI generates humans (60%) and voices (45%). MediaPET.ai is an AI video product built by MediaScience. — [TV Tech, May 21 2026](https://www.tvtechnology.com/insights/analysis/study-ai-labeling-does-not-hurt-video-ad-performance)
- [Meta policy] Ads created or significantly altered with Meta's generative AI tools carry an "AI info" label, either in the three-dot menu or next to "Sponsored". If the AI content includes a photorealistic human, the label always appears next to "Sponsored". — [Small Business Trends: Meta AI ad transparency](https://smallbiztrends.com/meta-ai-ad-transparency/); [Meta Transparency Center: labeling AI content](https://transparency.meta.com/governance/tracking-impact/labeling-ai-content)

### Inferences
- NXUS sells AI, so *some* AI is on-brand. But the thing the AI should demonstrate is the product (the WhatsApp agent answering), not fake people or a synthetic narrator. AI faces and voices are exactly what consumers most want labeled and most distrust (60% and 45%).
- Priority test: the founder or a real NXUS team member on camera (phone-shot, captioned, in Spanish), plus a screen recording of the agent answering a real-looking WhatsApp conversation at 11:48 PM. Run it against the current AI-VO ad as a head-to-head concept test.
- If AI voice is kept, use a natural Mexican-Spanish voice (not "Mark", which sounds US/English-branded) for the MX audience. Consider saying it openly: "Esta voz la hizo una IA, igual que la que contestaría a tus clientes", which turns the AI into a proof point rather than a deception.
- For the US Spanish-speaking audience, a neutral Latin American Spanish voice works. The English version needs a separate script, not a translation.

### Gaps
- No direct data on B2B decision-makers' reactions to AI voiceovers in ads. No Mexico-specific AI-ad perception data was found.
- The Meta study behind Motion's 32% figure was not verified.

## Andromeda-era creative diversity: what counts as different, how many, refresh cadence, fatigue signals

### Takeaway
Meta's retrieval system (Andromeda) rewards *meaningfully* different creatives (different concept, persona, angle, format or visual) and clusters near-duplicates so they compete with each other. Copy changes on the same visual barely count. Meta gives no official ad count. Practitioners suggest roughly 6-15+ genuinely distinct creatives per consolidated ad set, refreshed every 2-4 weeks, with fatigue flagged by CTR down >10% week over week, CPA up >15%, or prospecting frequency of about 2.5-3+.

### Cited Findings
- [OPINION + Meta quotes] Jon Loomer (Oct 20 2025): diversity means noticeably different visuals, formats (video/static/carousel), aspect ratios, and text aimed at different personas, pain points or angles. "Unless they're distinctly different enough, any variation is unlikely to make a meaningful impact." Meta's line: "Creative diversification will help advertisers get the best results." Meta recommends unique primary text per ad. A single ad holds up to 10 media items (Flexible Format) and 5 primary texts/5 headlines. "Start small… a few meaningful variations." — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda-creative-diversification/)
- [OPINION] Meta dropped the old 6-ads-per-ad-set guidance in 2025, and advertisers now run 10, 20 or 50 ads per ad set. — [Jon Loomer: Meta Andromeda](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda/); [Jon Loomer podcast, Sep 3 2025](https://pubcast.jonloomer.com/creative-diversification-is-the-new-targeting/)
- [VENDOR] Segwise (2026): 8-12 conceptually distinct concepts per campaign, 2-3 variations each, with variation that differs in hook, format, emotional trigger or visual. Andromeda groups similar creatives under one "Entity ID", so they compete. Variation must be visual, audio or structural, and different copy on the same visual does not count. Claimed similarity thresholds (>60% suppression, <40% target) are third-party, not Meta-documented. — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)
- [VENDOR, unverified secondhand] A widely repeated test: one ad set with 25 diverse creatives beat five ad sets of five by 17% more conversions at 16% lower cost (attributed to Logical Position / Meta). — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026); [Pipeboard](https://pipeboard.co/guides/creative-volume-andromeda)
- [VENDOR] Refresh cadence: a 2-3 week cycle (down from 6+ weeks). Launch replacements before decline. Fatigue signals: CTR down >10% week over week, CPA up >15%, prospecting frequency >3.0, falling spend share without a budget change. — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026)
- [VENDOR] Frequency of 2.5-3.0 is where performance typically declines. Static ads fatigue 30-50% faster than video (refresh every 20-30 days vs 40-60 for video), per MHI, cited secondhand. — [Segwise static vs video](https://segwise.ai/blog/static-video-ratio-meta-ads)

### Inferences
- NXUS's three current videos are arguably **one concept** (the same AI-VO, stock/AI visuals, "24/7 attention" angle and CTA). Andromeda likely treats them as near-duplicates. Three hooks on the same body is a hook test, not creative diversity.
- A launch set of ~8-10 genuinely distinct creatives:
  1. Founder talking head (selfie, Spanish): "Le revisé el WhatsApp a 5 restaurantes de CDMX. Esto encontré."
  2. Screen-record demo: an AI agent answering a reservation at 11:48 PM, real-time, with captions.
  3. Static image: a WhatsApp screenshot with "14 mensajes sin responder. 11:48 PM." plus the CTA.
  4. Carousel: "5 fugas de dinero en restaurantes con sucursales" (reservas, pedidos WhatsApp, reseñas sin responder, no-shows, delivery commissions).
  5. Before/after split screen: an owner's phone at 8 AM with 23 unread vs an agent-handled inbox.
  6. Testimonial or case (once real): restaurant owner or manager on camera.
  7. Text-on-screen "listicle" video with no VO, made for sound-off.
  8. Persona variant for non-restaurant "empresas grandes" (lead response time, sales follow-up).
  9. An English US-market variant (separate concept, not a dub).
  10. A "diagnóstico" explainer: what happens in the 30 minutes (screen of the deliverable).
- At small budgets, fewer, more distinct creatives in one consolidated ad set beats many variants across fragmented ad sets.

### Gaps
- No official Meta number of creatives, and no Meta documentation of similarity thresholds.
- No B2B-lead-gen-specific fatigue cadence data. Most of it is DTC.

## Static images, carousels vs video; formats (testimonial, demo, PAS, before-after); showing the product

### Takeaway
Evidence conflicts. Video tends to win on attention and upper-funnel metrics, while static often wins on CPA in direct response, B2B/SaaS and retargeting, and is far cheaper to produce. One aggregated analysis recommends ~70% static for SaaS/tech and for accounts under $20K/month. For NXUS, a mixed portfolio with a strong static share plus product-demo video is defensible, and showing the actual product (WhatsApp agent in action) is recommended across sources.

### Cited Findings
- [DATA, secondhand] An analysis of 67,000 Meta ads from 100+ top accounts (Curtis Howland / MisfitMarketing, 2025) found a median mix of ~61% static, 39% video. Suggested ratios: under $20K/month spend, 70% static/30% video; SaaS/Tech vertical, 70% static/30% video (ATTN). Video CTR 1.9% vs static 1.1%, but video CPA $48.20 vs static $34.50 (ATTN benchmarks). Only 40% of top accounts used carousels. — [Segwise, Mar 2026](https://segwise.ai/blog/static-video-ratio-meta-ads)
- [VENDOR] Static said to beat video on CPA by 20-35% in B2B, retargeting and price-driven promotions. Another vendor claims video wins by 15-30% on CPL for B2B/high-consideration offers. These conflict. — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/static-image-ads-when-they-outperform-video); [Stackmatix static vs video by industry](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/static-vs-video-ads-meta)
- [OPINION] B2B formats that work: product demos (short walkthroughs), founder/expert explainers, testimonials. — [Growth Spree](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)
- [OPINION] For click-to-WhatsApp, creative should show what the user gets by tapping, rather than a logo and tagline. Open with the most compelling moment. — [360dialog CTWA best practices](https://www.360dialog.com/blog/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp-best-practices)

### Inferences
- The single most persuasive asset for NXUS is likely a real screen recording of its agent answering a WhatsApp reservation or order at night, because it is proof, not a claim. It also fits a click-to-WhatsApp ad perfectly: "Escríbenos ahora y prueba cómo te contestaría tu propio agente". The prospect can experience the product in the click.
- Problem-agitate-solve is the current script structure, and it should be kept. But move the "solve" (the demo) up to ~5-8 s.

### Gaps
- No credible public data on carousel vs video for high-ticket B2B services lead gen.

## Primary text, headlines, click-to-message (WhatsApp) copy and ice breakers

### Takeaway
Little hard data exists on text length. Meta wants unique primary text per ad, and practitioners say to front-load the first line (shown before "…more"). For click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA), match the creative's promise to the ice breakers and the first automated reply, and tell people explicitly what to type or ask.

### Cited Findings
- [Meta guidance via Loomer] Use unique primary text per ad, with significant differences. Ads accept up to 5 primary texts and 5 headlines. — [Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda-creative-diversification/)
- [OPINION] Ice breakers are pre-set questions a user taps to open the chat. Predict the questions people will ask and write ice breakers for them. Map each ad's angle to a matching ice breaker. The first reply should continue the ad's promise, not restart the conversation. — [Blip help](https://help.blip.ai/hc/en-us/articles/20178934582551-How-to-create-a-Click-To-WhatsApp-Ad-using-Ads-manager-Meta); [Playbook newsletter](https://playbooknewsletter.beehiiv.com/p/the-fastest-whatsapp-funnels); [360dialog](https://www.360dialog.com/blog/click-to-whatsapp-best-practices)
- [OPINION] Generic brand creative with a button underneath makes the jump to chat feel disconnected. CTWA supports image, video, carousel and slideshow. WhatsApp Status ads were announced June 16 2025. Respect user boundaries after the click and get a clear marketing opt-in. — [360dialog](https://www.360dialog.com/blog/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp-best-practices); [Enrich Labs 2026 guide](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/whatsapp-advertising-complete-guide-2026); [Ominiflow](https://ominiflow.com/blog/whatsapp-click-to-whatsapp-ads-best-practices)
- [Search summary] I found no sourced data on the effect of primary text or headline length for B2B lead gen. — [search across vendor blogs](https://www.gtm8020.com/blog/facebook-ads-statistics-b2b)

### Inferences (copy templates for NXUS)
- Primary text, first line (hook): "¿Cuántos mensajes de WhatsApp recibe tu restaurante después de cerrar?" Then 2-3 short lines: what the agent does (reservas, pedidos, preguntas de menú, reseñas), proof once available, and the CTA: "Escríbenos 'DIAGNÓSTICO' y te decimos en 30 min dónde estás perdiendo ventas." Use emojis sparingly (one or two functional ones, such as 📲 or 🕛). There is no data either way.
- Headline: "Diagnóstico gratis de 30 min para restaurantes". Description: "Sin compromiso · CDMX y EE. UU."
- Ice breakers (MX): "¿Qué incluye el diagnóstico?" / "¿Funciona con mi sistema de reservas o POS?" / "Quiero ver cómo contestaría mi agente" / "¿Cuánto cuesta implementarlo?" The first reply should be the agent itself demonstrating, then qualifying: "¿Cuántas sucursales tienes?" "¿Cuántos mensajes recibes al día?"
- Use the CTWA conversation itself as the demo. The agent that answers the ad lead *is* the product.

### Gaps
- No Meta-published CTWA conversion benchmarks were retrieved. Text-length effects are unknown, so test them.

## Offers for high-ticket B2B: free diagnostic framing, scarcity, over-promising; restaurant angles

### Takeaway
Little rigorous 2025-2026 data exists on "free audit" offers or scarcity specifically. Competitor restaurant-AI vendors in LATAM lead with concrete outcomes (reservations, WhatsApp orders, reminders reducing no-shows, POS integration), and their numbers are unverified marketing claims. NXUS should make the diagnostic tangible (what you get), keep scarcity truthful, and avoid unsubstantiated percentage claims.

### Cited Findings
- [VENDOR] LATAM restaurant WhatsApp-AI vendors promise to take reservations, check availability, load them into the calendar, and send reminders and confirmations (Cliengo). Others integrate with the reservation system and the POS for delivery orders, and claim a "-40% no-shows" from WhatsApp reminders (GuruSup, unverified). Voice agents claim to handle 60-80% of restaurant calls at $99-499/month per location. — [Cliengo WhatsApp para restaurantes](https://guiawabusiness.cliengo.com/whatsapp-restaurantes); [GuruSup chatbot restaurantes](https://gurusup.com/es/chatbot-restaurantes); [CloudTalk voz IA restaurantes](https://www.cloudtalk.io/es/agente-de-voz-ia-para-restaurantes/); [Aurora Inbox, Feb 2026](https://www.aurorainbox.com/2026/02/11/chatbot-restaurantes-whatsapp/); [Aunoa](https://aunoa.ai/sectores/chatbots-para-restaurantes)
- [OPINION] A good CTR with poor lead quality points to the offer or targeting, not the video. Judge on cost per *qualified* lead. — [Growth Spree](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/meta-video-ads-b2b-saas-2026)

### Inferences
- "Diagnóstico gratis de 30 minutos" is a weak offer on its own, because it signals a sales call. Make it a deliverable: "Te entregamos un reporte con cuántos mensajes y reservas pierdes fuera de horario + un demo de tu propio agente con tu menú." A specific output beats "gratis".
- The competitive landscape is crowded with cheap SaaS chatbots ($99-499/month). NXUS should differentiate on custom work, multi-branch chains, and integration (POS, reservations, delivery apps, reviews), not on "chatbot 24/7", which competitors already own.
- Use scarcity ("cupos limitados") only if it is real (e.g., "Esta semana: 5 diagnósticos"). Fake urgency damages trust in high-ticket B2B. Avoid ROI claims ("+40% ventas") without case data, both for credibility and for Meta ad-policy review.
- Restaurant angles to test as separate concepts: after-hours reservations; WhatsApp orders and delivery commissions (selling direct vs aggregator apps); unanswered Google reviews; no-shows and reminders; multi-branch consistency.

### Gaps
- No primary data on scarcity or free-audit conversion lift in B2B Meta ads.
- I did not review the Meta Ad Library directly for competitors' current creatives. Recommend a manual check of Ad Library searches ("restaurantes WhatsApp IA", "agente IA restaurante", Cliengo, GuruSup, Aurora Inbox, Treble) in Mexico.
